# Professional Post

1. Identify the audience, single useful takeaway, evidence, voice, platform constraints, and desired response.
2. Preserve the user's facts and level of certainty. Do not manufacture results, quotes, clients, expertise, or personal experience.
3. Open with the concrete insight rather than engagement bait. Use short paragraphs and descriptive links or alt-text prompts.
4. Explain what happened, why it matters, and what the reader can apply.
5. Use hashtags sparingly and never add endorsements or calls to action the user did not request.
6. Flag confidential, legal, employer-policy, or attribution concerns before the draft is published.

Return one polished draft plus a compact fact-check list. Do not publish it.
